Cloud Architecture /
Builder Platform

01
PROBLEM

– The builder was a freeform canvas that assumed deep cloud infrastructure knowledge. Non-expert users stalled out or made errors that only surfaced after deployment, creating a dependency on senior DevOps engineers for architectures that should have been routine.

SOLUTION

– Replaced the open canvas with a guided, sequential workflow: plain-language intake, inline validation at each step, then a generated architecture — so the interface caught mistakes before they became deployments instead of after.

OUTCOME
35% FASTER ARCHITECTURE CREATION — VIA STREAMLINED, GUIDED WORKFLOWS REPLACING A FREEFORM CANVAS
20% FEWER USER ERRORS — CLEARER INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE PLUS INLINE VALIDATION AT EACH STEP
↓DEV REDUCED DEPENDENCY ON SENIOR DEVOPS ENGINEERS BY SIMPLIFYING COMPLEX TECHNICAL WORKFLOWS
– FEATURED CASE STUDY SAAS · AI HIRING

Replacing manual with
AI matching

02
PROBLEM

– Every application landed in the same queue, and the HR team manually screened each one before a candidate heard anything back. The wait killed engagement — good candidates dropped off before a human ever got to their profile, and the manual load only grew with volume.

SOLUTION

– Redesigned the flow around an AI matching engine that ranks and shortlists candidates against role criteria automatically, paired with faster status feedback so candidates weren't left guessing. HR moved from screening every application to reviewing a ranked shortlist.

OUTCOME
300% HIGHER USER RETENTION POST-REDESIGN, DRIVEN BY FASTER FEEDBACK AND A CLEARER CANDIDATE JOURNEY
50% LESS MANUAL LOAD ON THE HR TEAM — AI MATCHING REMOVED FIRST-PASS SCREENING FROM THEIR QUEUE
AI MATCHING ENGINE DESIGNED AND SHIPPED END-TO-END, FROM INTAKE THROUGH RANKED SHORTLIST
– FEATURED CASE STUDY ENTERPRISE · ESG REPORTING

Getting ESG reporting
out of spreadsheets

03
PROBLEM

– ESG documentation lived across scattered Excel files, manually reconciled by hand every reporting cycle. The process was slow, error-prone, and nobody on the team fully trusted the numbers by the time they reached the final report.

SOLUTION

– Designed a structured collect-to-report pipeline: guided digital forms replaced scattered sheets, data validated and auto-compiled on entry, and a report generated straight from verified data — no manual spreadsheet merge at the end.

OUTCOME
70% LESS MANUAL EXCEL WORK — STRUCTURED INTAKE REPLACED SPREADSHEET RECONCILIATION
40% HIGHER USER SATISFACTION — REPORTED AFTER THE GUIDED WORKFLOW REPLACED THE OLD PROCESS
50% FASTER REPORTING CYCLE — FROM DATA COLLECTION THROUGH TO A SUBMITTABLE ESG DOCUMENT
